保密协议（中文示例）

一、定义

"保密信息"指甲方（设计提供方）与乙方（微木品牌管理（深圳）有限公司及其代工生产团队）在合作过程中，一方向另一方披露的，与手表设计方案、CAD 图纸、模具结构、材质配方、报价、客户信息等相关的任何非公开信息，无论以书面、口头、电子或其他形式披露。

二、保密义务

  1. 双方承诺仅在本次手表设计/代工合作范围内使用对方披露的保密信息，不得用于本协议目的之外的任何用途。
  2. 双方不得向任何第三方披露、公开或转让保密信息，除非事先获得披露方书面同意。
  3. 双方应采取不低于其保护自身同等重要商业信息的谨慎程度，保护所获得的保密信息。

三、设计知识产权归属

  1. 由设计提供方原创并提交的设计稿、CAD 图纸、效果图等，其知识产权归设计提供方所有，代工方仅获得为完成本次生产目的所必需的使用许可。
  2. 若代工方在现有模具库基础上提供结构性建议或工艺改良，该结构性公模基础部分的知识产权仍归代工方所有；双方应在正式合作前就新增的定制化设计部分的权属另行书面约定。
  3. 未经书面许可，任何一方不得将对方的设计方案或模具结构用于与第三方的其他合作。

四、有效期限

本协议自双方签署之日起生效，保密义务持续至合作结束后 两（2）年（具体期限可由双方协商调整）。涉及商业秘密的信息，保密义务持续至该信息进入公开领域为止。

NDA Template (English Sample)

1. Definitions

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party — the design provider ("Party A") or Weimu Brand Management (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. and its manufacturing team ("Party B") — relating to watch design concepts, CAD drawings, mold structures, material formulations, pricing, or client information, whether disclosed in written, verbal, electronic, or other form.

2. Confidentiality Obligations

  1. Both Parties agree to use the Confidential Information disclosed by the other Party solely within the scope of this watch design/manufacturing collaboration, and not for any purpose outside this agreement.
  2. Neither Party shall disclose, publish, or transfer Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.
  3. Both Parties shall exercise a degree of care no less than that used to protect their own confidential business information of similar importance.

3. Ownership of Design IP

  1. Original designs, CAD drawings, and renderings submitted by the design provider remain the intellectual property of the design provider; the manufacturer is granted only the license necessary to complete the agreed production.
  2. Where the manufacturer contributes structural suggestions or process improvements based on its existing mold library, ownership of the underlying mold structure remains with the manufacturer; ownership of any newly customized design elements should be agreed separately in writing prior to formal collaboration.
  3. Neither Party may use the other Party's design plans or mold structures for collaboration with any third party without prior written consent.

4. Term

This Agreement takes effect upon signature by both Parties and the confidentiality obligations shall remain in effect for two (2) years after the end of the collaboration (the specific term may be adjusted by mutual agreement). For information constituting a trade secret, the confidentiality obligation continues until such information enters the public domain.
